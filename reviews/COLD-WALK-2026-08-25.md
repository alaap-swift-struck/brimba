# The cold walk — a real first run, 2026-08-25

`first_run_review`'s criterion 10 asks whether anyone has actually walked a fresh
account. Until today nobody had, and no commit could buy it — the review scored
that criterion 0 in both rounds and said so plainly. The owner signed up on staging
with an unused address and captured all three screens. This is that evidence.

## What actually happened

**1 · Sign in.** "Welcome to Brimba", six boxes for the emailed code, the address
shown back, `Change email` and `Resend code` beneath, and a toast: "Code sent —
check your email." One job, nothing to work out. The review scored the sign-up path
95 and the walk agrees.

**2 · Profile.** "Set up your profile" — *"Tell us who you are — your team gets
created right after."* Avatar drop zone, first and last name, one button. The button
reads **"Creating your team..."** while it works, which is the right thing: it names
what is happening rather than spinning.

Worth protecting: that subtitle is the ONLY place in the entire flow that tells a
new person what happens next. It is carrying the whole journey alone.

**3 · Home — where it stops helping.** The landing screen shows the team name, an
"Admin · 1 member" line, and exactly two cards: **Team** (members, roles and
invites) and **Settings** (your account and teams). The sidebar offers Home,
Learning, Help and Settings. **The bottom two-thirds of the screen is empty.**

Nothing says what to do first. Learning and Help — the two things this base is
actually FOR — are four words in a sidebar and are not mentioned on the screen a new
owner lands on. Neither is the assistant. Neither is importing. The review's static
finding was exact, and the screenshot is the proof: *the string "get started" does
not exist anywhere in the app.*

## The verdict, in the owner's terms

Nothing was broken. Nothing crashed. No empty screen read as an error — the 95%
"guarded" figure holds up in the flesh. But a person who has just created a team is
left to work out what this is for from four sidebar words, and the largest area of
their first screen is blank.

That is the 3-of-21 "helpful" measurement seen from the inside: **the app is careful,
and it is not welcoming.**

## What this unlocks

Criterion 10 was 0 for want of a human and is now earned. The remaining move is the
first-run prompt on `/home` — +6.35 by the review's own arithmetic — and it is being
built against this evidence rather than against a guess.
